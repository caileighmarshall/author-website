A Note From Evelyn

Writing has always been my way of exploring the human heart. From small-town romances to sweeping city sagas, my goal is to capture the messy, beautiful magic of falling in love.

I believe every soul has a story worth telling. My journey started in a quiet library in Vermont, and now I have the privilege of sharing my worlds with readers across the globe. When I'm not at my typewriter, you can find me tending to my lavender garden or hunting for the perfect vintage postcard.

Yours truly, Evelyn Thorne
