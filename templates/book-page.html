Whispers in the Rain

Contemporary Romance calendar_today Published October 2023 star star star star star_half 4.8 (1.2k reviews)

He was a architect who only built walls around his heart. She was a landscape artist who knew that even the toughest soil could bloom with enough care. When a storm strands them in a remote cabin in the Pacific Northwest, the only thing more dangerous than the elements is the truth they've both been hiding.

Julian Vance doesn't do "forever." Maya Sterling doesn't do "temporary." But as the rain drums against the roof, the whispers of what could be become too loud to ignore. In this heart-wrenching tale of second chances and hidden scars, two souls learn that sometimes the best way to move forward is to let the rain wash everything else away.
