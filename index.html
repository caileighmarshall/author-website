About Caileigh

Caileigh Marshall is a software engineer who writes swoonworthy stories about finding love in unlikely places. When not at work, she can be found exploring antique shops, sewing/crafting, watching documentaries, or dreaming about her next vacation.

Caileigh is represented by Matthew Valdez at Megibow Literary Agency.
