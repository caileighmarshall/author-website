About Caileigh

Caileigh Marshall is a software engineer who writes stories about women finding love in unlikely places. When not at work, she can be found exploring antique shops, sewing/crafting, watching documentaries, or dreaming about her next vacation. You can join her newsletter here!

Caileigh is represented by Matthew Valdez at Megibow Literary Agency.
